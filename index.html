Rules of the shoot

  1. You cover transport within Osogbo. No shoots outside Osogbo for now.
  2. Part payment is required to validate your booking.
  3. Book at least three days before your birthday or occasion.
  4. No raw pictures. Only images you have paid for are delivered.

  7. Editing takes 2 to 3 days. Express delivery of 5 pictures is an extra ₦10,000.
  8. Hotel or location fees for your shoot are paid by you.
  9. No refunds. Prices are not negotiable.
